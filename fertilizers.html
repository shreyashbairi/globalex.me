Fertilizers

Top-quality fertilizers — Urea-B (N46), Potash, Ammonia, Ammonium Nitrate and NPK — designed for optimal crop growth, reliably sourced from Turkmenistan, Uzbekistan, Kazakhstan and Azerbaijan.

Urea B (N46)

N46 · 46% Nitrogen Bulk · Prilled · Granular · Liquid
Nitrogen Fertilizer

A widely used nitrogen fertilizer known for its high nutrient content. A white, crystalline substance that is highly soluble in water, providing slow and steady release of nitrogen which can improve crop yields and reduce nitrogen runoff. Supplied in bulk, prilled, granular and liquid forms.
