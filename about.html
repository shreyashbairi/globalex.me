About Us

An international trading house built in Dubai. Sourcing from the Caspian, delivering worldwide.

Established in 2019 based on honesty, integrity, trust and a vision to succeed. We specialize in Urea, Sulphur, Fertilizers, as well as Polymers — registered as a freezone company under DMCC.

Who We Are?

A trading partner committed to quality, transparency and the long game.

Globalex Trading DMCC operates from Dubai's Jumeirah Lakes Towers as a freezone licensed company. Since 2019, we've built our practice on three principles: source from origins we trust, deliver with the rigor our clients expect, and never compromise on the quality of what we move.

Our products originate mostly from the Caspian Region and Central Asian markets — Turkmenistan, Uzbekistan, Kazakhstan, and Azerbaijan — with additional sourcing partnerships in the UAE, Saudi Arabia and China.

Headquarters
Jumeirah Lakes Towers · Dubai

Operating from Dubai's premier freezone — the bridge between East and West.

Our office at 2605 X3 Tower sits inside Cluster X of Jumeirah Lakes Towers, one of the world's most active commercial freezones. The location is deliberate: Dubai gives us the regulatory clarity of DMCC, the logistics depth of Jebel Ali and DP World, and the geographic position to broker between Caspian producers and global buyers.

This is where shipments are negotiated, contracts are signed, and the relationships that anchor 20+ trade lanes are nurtured.
