Polymers

Trade across the polymer spectrum — polyethylene (LDPE, HDPE, LLDPE, UHMWPE), polypropylene homopolymer and copolymer, and the chemical additives that tune performance to your application.

Polyethylene

LDPE HDPE LLDPE UHMWPE
Versatile Polyolefin

A versatile and widely used polymer. Low-density polyethylene (LDPE) suits packaging films and plastic bags; high-density polyethylene (HDPE) is the workhorse for pipes, containers, and automotive parts. Linear low-density (LLDPE) and ultra-high molecular weight (UHMWPE) round out the family for specialty films and engineering applications.

Polypropylene

Homopolymer Copolymer
Lightweight & Durable

A lightweight, durable, and versatile material used in packaging, automotive parts, textiles, and consumer goods. We supply both homopolymer (rigid, single-monomer grades) and copolymer (impact-modified, multi-monomer grades).

Additives

Plasticizers UV stabilizers Flame retardants Antimicrobials
Performance Tuning

Chemical compounds added to polymers to improve their performance — including plasticizers, UV stabilizers, flame retardants and antimicrobial additives. Specified by application, supplied to grade.
